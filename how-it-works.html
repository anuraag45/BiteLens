How BiteLens Works

From complex ingredient label to clear decision in four transparent steps.

Behind the Process

Understanding what happens during each phase.

Phase A: High-Accuracy Label Recognition

Package labels in India vary widely in print quality and formatting. BiteLens uses adaptive text extraction specifically tuned for FSSAI-style back-of-pack layouts.

Phase B: INS Database & Heuristic Matching

The extracted codes (e.g. INS 500(ii)) map directly to standardized functional classes, explaining whether an ingredient acts as an emulsifier, acidity regulator, or preservative.

Phase C: Transparent Dual Heuristics

Rather than hiding logic behind a black box, BiteLens scores processing level (NOVA) alongside macronutrient ratios so you know exactly why a score was assigned.
