1. The NOVA Classification Framework

Pink’s Health Score utilizes the internationally recognized NOVA Food Classification System, developed by researchers at the University of São Paulo. NOVA groups food items into four distinct categories based on the extent and purpose of industrial processing:

NOVA Group 1: Unprocessed

Fresh fruits, raw grains, vegetables, fresh milk, and eggs.

NOVA Group 2: Culinary Ingredients

Oils, butter, sugar, and salt used to prepare meals.

NOVA Group 3: Processed Foods

Canned vegetables, freshly baked artisanal breads, cured meats.

NOVA Group 4: Ultra-Processed (UPFs)

Industrial formulations with emulsifiers, flavor enhancers, and additives.

2. Regulatory Landscape in India (FSSAI Context)

Independent Heuristic Notice

India does not currently have a finalized, mandatory front-of-pack nutrition rating standard. While the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has published draft proposals for front-of-pack labeling (FOPL), no single mandatory scoring format has been enacted into law nationwide.

Because of this ongoing regulatory state, Pink’s Health Score is explicitly presented as an independent, transparent heuristic—not an official FSSAI rating, and not a medical or diagnostic certification.
