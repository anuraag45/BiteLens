The Label Decoding Gap in India

While nutrition labels are mandatory in India, cryptic INS numbers and complex functional classes make true ingredients hard to understand. Most global tracking apps lack deep coverage for regional packaged products. Pink bridges this gap by decoding ingredients into clear, everyday language.
