About BiteLens

Our mission is to translate complex ingredient lists and industrial additive codes into plain language so consumers never have to guess what they’re eating.

Why We Built BiteLens

Bridging the Label Decoding Gap in India

When shopping for packaged groceries in India, reading the back of a container often feels like trying to decipher a chemistry paper. Essential information is buried behind technical INS numbers (like INS 621 or INS 322), fine print obscures added sugars, and generic health marketing on the front box rarely reflects actual processing levels.

BiteLens was conceived as an academic Software Group Project (SGP) deliverable to demonstrate how mobile optical recognition, plain-language translation algorithms, and transparent dual heuristics can empower consumers with real-time clarity.

Plain-Language First

We believe food literacy shouldn't require a biochemistry degree. Every additive code is translated into plain, everyday English.

Scientific Transparency

Our Health Score draws on the international NOVA framework and published FSSAI regulations—never unsourced marketing claims.

Dual Scoring Model

We separate processing level from macronutrient goals so consumers see transparent trade-offs without hidden black-box scores.
