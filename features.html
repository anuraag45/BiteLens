App Features

Designed to bring full transparency to Indian packaged food labels through plain-language decoding and dual scoring.

Barcode & Label Scanning

Point your camera at any packaged food item in India. Pink supports rapid barcode lookup as well as Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to photograph and parse back-of-pack ingredient lists directly from the package.

Plain-Language Ingredient Decoding

No more deciphering cryptic INS additive numbers (like INS 621 or INS 211) or technical chemical names. Pink automatically translates functional classes, thickeners, and preservatives into clear, everyday English explanations.

Health Score

An informational heuristic score based on processing level (informed by the NOVA framework) and nutrient thresholds (saturated fat, added sugar, and sodium). This score provides a quick view of processing quality. Note: This is a transparent heuristic, not an official or medical rating.

Goal Fit Score

Evaluates how well a product aligns with your personal macro and fitness goals (weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance). Crucially, Pink presents this score separately from the Health Score—they are never merged—so you can easily spot foods that fit a protein target yet remain ultra-processed.

Additive Flagging

Identifies additives present in scanned foods and highlights their functional purpose alongside regulatory context. Every flag is explicitly tied to disclosed regulatory guidelines or published scientific sources.

Nutritional Dashboard

A clean, centralized overview displaying calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, and active additive flags for all scanned items. Keeps your daily intake organized without clutter.

Dual Scoring Principle

Pink deliberately keeps the Health Score (processing & nutrient quality) distinct from the Goal Fit Score (macronutrient alignment). Merging them into a single score can obscure important trade-offs—for instance, a protein bar might score high on your muscle-building goal while scoring lower on processing level.
